## Major areas of AI (following AIMA)

- **Problem solving and search** — finding sequences of actions that reach a goal.
- **Knowledge, reasoning, and planning** — representing what is known and deriving new conclusions or plans.
- **Uncertain knowledge and reasoning** — probability, Bayesian networks, and decision making under uncertainty.
- **Machine learning** — learning patterns from data instead of hand-coding rules.
- **Communicating, perceiving, and acting** — natural language processing, computer vision, robotics.

## Where machine learning fits

Machine learning is one subfield of AI. Most recent practical progress (including deep learning and large language models) comes from ML, but many problems — scheduling, planning, optimization — also draw on search, reasoning, and decision-making methods.
